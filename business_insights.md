# Business Insights

- Cleaned dataset contains **7,500 unique order records** after removing exact duplicates.
- The project covers **800 unique customers** across five business regions.
- Total net sales: **₹198,854,948.37**
- Total profit: **₹34,867,417.88**
- Overall profit margin: **17.53%**
- Loss-making orders identified: **402**
- Highest-profit region: **South** with profit of **₹7,335,624.96**
- Highest-profit category: **Technology** with profit of **₹19,124,808.96**
- Weakest sub-category by total profit: **Paper** with **₹969,165.73**
- Highest-profit product: **Ultrabook** with profit of **₹2,229,961.43**
- Highest net-sales year: **2024** with **₹67,672,376.71**

## Business Recommendations

1. Review discounting on loss-making orders and sub-categories with weak margins.
2. Prioritize inventory and campaigns in the strongest regions and categories.
3. Track monthly sales and profit together rather than revenue alone.
4. Use customer-level net sales and profit to identify high-value customers.
5. Monitor profit margin alongside sales growth to avoid unprofitable expansion.
